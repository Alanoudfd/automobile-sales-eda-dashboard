# Automobile Sales Analysis & Interactive Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project explores historical automobile sales data to understand how economic recessions affect vehicle sales performance, advertising expenditure, consumer confidence, GDP trends, and unemployment rates.

Using Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Plotly, and Dash, the project combines exploratory data analysis (EDA) with an interactive dashboard to generate actionable business insights.

---

## Objectives

- Analyze automobile sales trends over time.
- Compare recession and non-recession periods.
- Investigate the relationship between advertising expenditure and sales.
- Study the impact of GDP, unemployment, and consumer confidence on automobile sales.
- Develop an interactive dashboard for data exploration.

## Key Insights

### Economic Recessions Significantly Reduce Vehicle Sales

Automobile sales consistently declined during recession periods, demonstrating the sensitivity of the automotive market to economic downturns.

### Vehicle Categories React Differently

Smaller and economy-focused vehicles maintained relatively stronger sales performance during recessions compared to larger vehicle segments.

### Advertising Expenditure Shows Stability

Marketing expenditure remained comparatively stable over time, while sales experienced larger fluctuations.

### Consumer Confidence Influences Sales

Lower consumer confidence levels were associated with reduced automobile purchases during recession periods.

### Unemployment Negatively Impacts Demand

Higher unemployment rates generally corresponded with lower automobile sales.

### Seasonal Effects Exist

Sales patterns showed recurring seasonal variations throughout the year.
